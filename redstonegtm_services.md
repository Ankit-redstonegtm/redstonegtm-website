## Complete GTM Services

### Full TAM Mapping and Account Universe Build
**Delivered.** An account-level map of your total addressable market in a centralized system, enriched with firmographics and company intelligence, with CRM accounts synchronized bidirectionally to stay in continuous agreement as a single source of truth.

**Value.** You see your whole market in one authoritative place that stays current, instead of fragmented lists going stale. Your TAM is always in sync with your CRM.

### Account Scoring and Prioritization
**Delivered.** A fit-scoring model derived from your closed-won patterns and ICP criteria, applied across your TAM with A/B/C grade tiers and synced back to your CRM, where the score automatically gates entry into campaigns and signal monitoring.

**Value.** Accounts ranked by fit, so your team's attention concentrates on who is most likely to convert, independent of timing. Sales motion becomes efficient and data-driven.

### Contact Sourcing and Data Coverage
**Delivered.** Multi-provider contact discovery and enrichment waterfalls with deduplication and hygiene logic, plus supplemental sources to capture contacts any single provider misses. On-demand enrichment for ad hoc accounts and prospects.

**Value.** Maximum contact coverage at controlled cost, with clean, verified people-level data available on demand. Your contact database grows intelligently without manual outreach lists.

### Persona Prioritization and Segmentation
**Delivered.** AI-powered analysis of each contact's title, profile, and work history to assign buying-committee priority tiers (P1 through P4/P5) and function-based personas (e.g., procurement, finance, HR), with industry tenure and relevant background extracted. All contacts enriched regardless of tier.

**Value.** Every contact ranked by influence and bucketed by role, so you can filter, report, and tailor messaging by buying-committee track. Your campaigns orchestrate to the right people at the right time.

### Campaign Orchestration and Automation
**Delivered.** The intelligent routing layer that takes scored accounts and prioritized contacts and moves them into the right motion: right message track, channel (email, LinkedIn, call list), sender, and timing. Includes criteria-gated CRM sync and multi-touch sequencing.

**Value.** Everything from your data strategy becomes coordinated action through one connected system rather than disconnected, generic sends. Campaigns run on autopilot with complete visibility.

### Signal Monitoring and Buying-Moment Alerts
**Delivered.** Continuous monitoring of your TAM for external buying-moment triggers: new hires, leadership changes, promotions, job transitions, funding events, M&A activity, tech-stack shifts, web re-engagement, and 10-K signals. Alerts surface accounts when a buying window opens.

**Value.** You know which accounts are in a buying moment and when. Live campaigns are timed to actual relevance instead of static cadences. The timing layer that complements fit scoring and drives reply rates.

### Account Research Automation
**Delivered.** Automated intelligence extraction and synthesis: 10-K signals (revenue targets, expansion plans, hiring, product launches, compliance risks), org and sales-team structure, territory mapping, contact backgrounds, and technographic details. Outputs include account summaries, reportable data points, and drafted intro messages synced to the CRM.

**Value.** Reps get fully researched, outreach-ready account profiles without manual prep work. Messaging becomes personalized and credible, and hours of daily research time are freed up for selling.

### Rep Enablement and Workflow Automation
**Delivered.** Orchestrated daily sales motion: prioritized morning briefings, pre-call prep packs, and post-call workflows that process call recordings (Gong integration) to extract signals and log buying-committee insights directly to the CRM.

**Value.** Reps focus on selling, not prepping or logging. First-party call intelligence stays actionable in the CRM, and coaching insights are automated.

### Long-Term CRM Hygiene and Data Freshness
**Delivered.** Continuously running workflows that maintain CRM health: automatic enrichment of new leads, duplicate detection and merging, data decay refreshes, and real-time capture of company changes (new hires, leadership shifts, funding, M&A). No manual intervention required.

**Value.** Your CRM stays perpetually fresh and complete without RevOps resource drain. Sales teams always work with current data, and your enrichment investment compounds over time.

### Custom ICP Identification and Lead Scoring
**Delivered.** Bespoke logic that identifies your Ideal Customer Profile through firmographic, technographic, and behavioral patterns. Custom scoring rules automatically categorize leads and contacts by purchase propensity, engagement likelihood, and fit, with real-time sync to CRM workflows and campaign gating.

**Value.** Segmentation and lead routing become intelligent and automated. Campaigns target only high-intent prospects. Your scoring evolves with your business and market without manual list management.

### LinkedIn Outbound and Direct Engagement
**Delivered.** Integrated LinkedIn outreach campaigns powered by your enriched CRM data: targeted sequences to decision-makers and influencers, with message personalization driven by company research and contact intelligence. Campaign performance is tracked and synced back to HubSpot for attribution.

**Value.** LinkedIn becomes a demand-generation channel, not a time sink. Sequences are hyper-personalized based on real company and contact data. Outbound motion is coordinated with email campaigns and sales calls for omnichannel engagement.
