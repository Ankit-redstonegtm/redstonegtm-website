EXAMPLE — Redstone GTM
TAM mapping checklist for a sales-led vertical SaaS company
This file is a stand-in lead magnet. It is not a client deliverable.

1. Name the motion
   - Who sells, to whom, and what "closed-won" has actually looked like.
   - One primary market. A second market is a second list.

2. Separate the ICP from the slogan
   - Which firmographics show up in wins.
   - Which attributes were assumed and do not survive the win data.
   - Which exclusions matter (size, geography, tech, business model).

3. Build the account universe
   - Sources beyond the CRM and beyond LinkedIn.
   - A rule for what counts as an account in this vertical.
   - A single list, with a stable id, that the CRM can stay aligned to.

4. Mark what you do not know
   - Coverage gaps by segment or geography.
   - Accounts in the CRM that are not in the market.
   - Accounts in the market that are not in the CRM.

5. Only then prioritize
   - Fit from the win pattern, independent of timing.
   - A tier the team will actually work.
   - A field the CRM and the campaigns can gate on.

6. Hand it to a rep
   - Can a new AE tell, from the record, whether an account is in the market?
   - Can they tell who to contact first, without a side spreadsheet?
   - If not, the map is not finished.
