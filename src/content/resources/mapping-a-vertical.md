## Situation

A sales-led company is about to hire account executives into a vertical. The current list is a mix of inbound, a data vendor export, and accounts a few reps already know. Nobody can say what share of the market that covers.

## Work

The engagement would map the account universe, separate fit from timing, and put a priority list in the CRM before the new hires start. Signals and outbound routing come after the market is defined, not before.

## What a published version will include

- The vertical, named by the client.
- What was true at kickoff, in their words.
- What the team had in hand at handoff: the map, the score, and the operating rhythm.
- A quotation only if the client wrote it.

Until that exists, this page stays an example.
