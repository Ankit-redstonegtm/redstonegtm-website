A signal is not a next action. A funding note in a Slack channel, a job post, or a line in a 10-K still leaves the rep to decide whether the account fits, who to contact, and what the first line should be.

The useful version is narrower:

1. The account is already inside the mapped market.
2. The change is one this sales motion treats as a reason to engage.
3. The CRM shows the fit, the person, and a first line the rep can edit.

Until those three are true, more alerts make the week noisier. The recording that belongs here will walk through one signal, end to end, on a real account shape without inventing a client result.
